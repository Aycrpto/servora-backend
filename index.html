Questions, answered

Everything you’d ask us before trusting us.

Is Servora really free for customers? +
Yes — completely. Browsing, searching, contacting pros and getting quotes costs nothing, and you don’t even need to create an account. You pay only your pro’s quoted price, only when the job is done. Servora earns from the professional side: a small commission on completed paid jobs, plus optional subscriptions pros buy for extra visibility.
Do I need to register to book someone? +
No. Just tell us the job and drop a phone number so pros can reach you with quotes. That’s it — no password, no profile, no email verification marathon. Your number is shared only with the pros you choose to contact.
How does Servora vet its professionals? +
Every pro passes a 4-stage check before their profile goes live: NIN/government ID verification, a practical skill assessment in their trade, two guarantor references, and an address confirmation. After that, their rating is earned job by job — and pros who fall below 4.2★ are removed from the platform.
What does “escrow-protected” actually mean? +
When you accept a quote, your payment goes into a secure holding account — not to the pro. The pro sees the job is funded (so they show up), but the money is only released when you confirm the work is complete. If there’s a dispute, our resolution team steps in within 24 hours.
What happens if the job goes wrong? +
Our Job-Done Guarantee covers every booking: report an issue within 7 days and we’ll send the pro back to fix it free of charge, send a different pro, or refund you. Around 97% of jobs are done right the first time — but when they’re not, you’re never the one holding the bill.
Which parts of Nigeria do you cover? +
Servora covers all 36 states and FCT Abuja. Coverage is deepest in Lagos and the South West (Oyo, Ogun, Osun), plus Abuja and Port Harcourt — and new pros are verified nationwide every day. If your state has few pros yet, post your job anyway: we’ll notify you the moment a verified pro near you joins, and you’ll get ₦2,000 off that first booking.
I’m a professional. How do I join and what does it cost? +
Tap “Become a Pro,” upload your ID and trade details, and complete verification — most pros are approved within 48 hours. Joining is free on the Starter plan. You pay commission only when a customer actually pays you through Servora — no job, no fee. Upgrade to Pro (₦7,500/mo) or Elite (₦25,000/mo) for priority placement, unlimited leads and lower commission. Active pros average 3–5 jobs per week.
