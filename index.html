How escrow works

Your money waits until the work is done.

Pay a stranger cash up front and you have no leverage if the job goes wrong. On Servora, payment sits with Servora — not the pro — and is released in stages you control.

  1. 1

    Accept an itemised quote

    Materials, labour and any inspection fee are listed separately, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.

  2. 2

    Pay into escrow

    You pay the quoted total to Servora, not to the pro. The pro can see the job is funded before they arrive.

  3. 3

    Approve the materials

    The pro photographs the site before starting. Once you approve, the material cost is released so they can buy what’s needed.

  4. 4

    Confirm the job is done

    The labour fee is released only when you confirm. Not happy? The pro gets one redo. If it’s still wrong, Servora reviews the photos and decides whether to release the fee or refund it.

Payments open at launch. Until then, nothing on Servora takes or moves money.

Questions, answered

Everything you’d ask us before trusting us.

Is Servora free for customers? +
Yes. Browsing, searching and messaging professionals cost nothing, and you never need an account. You pay only your pro’s quoted price. Servora earns a 12% commission from professionals on jobs they’ve been paid for.
Do I need an account? +
No. Customers never need an account, a password or an email address. You only share your phone number when you choose to message a professional.
How does Servora check professionals? +
Before a professional appears in search, they upload a government ID — NIN slip, driver’s licence or international passport — and take a live selfie. We match the selfie to the ID photo; if the automated check isn’t conclusive, a person reviews it. Professionals who don’t pass aren’t listed.
What does “escrow” mean here? +
When you accept a quote, you pay the total into an escrow account held by Servora — not to the professional. The material cost is released once you approve it, so they can buy what’s needed. The labour fee is released only when you confirm the job is done. Payments open at launch; until then, nothing on Servora takes or moves money.
What happens if the job goes wrong? +
The professional gets one chance to redo the work. If you’re still not satisfied, Servora reviews the before-and-after photos they uploaded and decides whether to release the labour fee to them or refund it to you.
Which parts of Nigeria do you cover? +
Professionals in any of the 36 states and the FCT can join. Servora is pre-launch, so whether there’s a professional near you depends on who has joined so far.
I’m a professional. How do I join, and what does it cost? +
Pro sign-up opens at launch. Until then you can join the waitlist with your name, phone number, trade and city — no documents needed. When sign-up opens, you create a profile and verify your identity with a government ID and a live selfie. Joining is free. Servora takes a 12% commission, and only on jobs you’ve been paid for.
